Cleaned Neatly LLC was built from a simple belief: your workplace deserves to be treated with the same care you'd give your own home. We are a locally owned and operated commercial cleaning company based in St. Louis, Missouri — and every facility we clean gets our full attention, every single visit.

We work with offices, medical facilities, retail locations, auto dealerships, schools, and more across the St. Louis metro area. No matter the size of your space, you receive the same professional, detail-oriented service we'd want for ourselves.

What sets us apart isn't just the cleaning — it's the relationship. You'll always know who is coming, when they're coming, and exactly what they're taking care of. We keep the lines of communication open and respond to any concern within the same day.
